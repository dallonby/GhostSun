# Thermal behaviour in sunlight (2026-08-02, machined aluminium case)

Summary and design rules in FOLDS.md thermal section.

```
=== 1. uniform soak ===
all-Al bench + Al mirrors: focus and alignment invariant (spacings and focal lengths scale together).
  grating on BK7 (Shelyak 2400): tuned line drifts 4.66 pm/K (11 K per core width). GhostSun already tracks the line: retune is a knob touch, and the drift doubles as a free case thermometer.
  grating on fused silica (holographic): tuned line drifts 0.34 pm/K (147 K per core width). GhostSun already tracks the line: retune is a knob touch, and the drift doubles as a free case thermometer.
  flats (fused silica substrate in Al cells): pointing drift only; fold_tol.py shows pointing DOFs cost no blur.

=== 2. side-to-side gradients (the real budget) ===
tilt between elements L apart on a case W wide, per kelvin of side-to-side difference: alpha * L / W
DOF                                            deg/K  allowable dT
BUDGET  OAP2 pitch (Lc rail 150)              0.0010        27.3 K
BUDGET  grating clock (rotor seat)            0.0006        80.3 K
PROD    OAP1 pitch (collimated arm 330)       0.0018        27.6 K
PROD    OAP2 pitch (Lc rail 270)              0.0015        18.9 K
PROD    grating in-plane yaw (arm 330)        0.0018        33.1 K
Verdict: ~10-25 K of SUSTAINED side-to-side difference eats the +3 um budget. A dark case in full sun develops 5-15 K untreated, <3 K painted white / foil-wrapped. So: white or bare-metal exterior, no black anodize outside, and let it soak 15-20 min after slewing.

=== 3. local loads ===
  slit jaws absorb ~2.4 W -> tower rises ~3.0 K -> slit despace ~4 um (tolerance 210 um): fine.
  polished/tilted jaws (already 10 deg) reflect most of it back out the snout; an ERF halves everything.
  mirrors see mW after the slit: figure distortion is nm-class on aluminium substrates. Ignore.
  periscope column (production): a vertical gradient tilts the lift flats -> pure image shift (fold_tol: no blur). Benign.

=== recommendations ===
  1. Machined Al case, all-Al mounts and OAPs: keeps the athermal property. Avoid steel standoffs in the beam train (CTE mismatch turns soak into tilt).
  2. Exterior: white paint or bare metal; never black.
  3. Thermal break (G10 washers or a printed collar) between the snout/telescope flange and the case front wall: the OTA is the hottest thing you touch.
  4. Use GhostSun's live line-position readout as the thermometer: 4.7 pm/K (BK7) means a 10-pm line drift flags a 2 K soak change before any blur is visible.
  5. Soak 15-20 min after setup; expect to touch the grating tune knob once per session, nothing else.
```
